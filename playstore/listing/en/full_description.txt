Tachimetro is a no-frills GPS speedometer: it shows your current speed full-screen, in huge, high-contrast digits designed to be read at a glance while your phone is mounted in a car or on a motorbike.

NOTHING ELSE
No menus, no ads, no charts. The app's only animation is the charging icon filling up, the only splash of color (lime) in an otherwise black-and-white interface. Just the speed number, always visible and always accurate, in every lighting condition.

KEY FEATURES
• Real-time GPS speed, updated every second
• Full-screen digits that auto-scale to fit the width
• Session max speed, always visible; the "Reset" button clears both max speed and distance
• "Keep screen on" toggle, with a smart default based on charging state
• Immersive fullscreen display: no status or navigation bar to distract you
• Automatic "no GPS signal" detection when the signal is lost
• Trip distance since the last reset, shown in the bottom-right corner, measured while the app is open and saved between sessions
• Charging indicator: animated lightning icon, visible only while the phone is charging

ANDROID AUTO
• Your current speed also appears on the car's Android Auto screen, updated every second just like on the phone
• When the GPS signal drops, the car screen shows "Searching for signal..." instead of a stale value
• If location permission hasn't been granted yet, you can grant it starting from the car screen — no need to open the app on your phone first
• While Android Auto is connected the phone stops keeping the screen on and shows a neutral message; on disconnect it goes back to exactly how it was
• The car screen's look (text size and style) is controlled by Android Auto itself, as with every compatible app

PRIVACY FIRST
Tachimetro does not request Internet access: no data ever leaves your phone. GPS location is used solely to compute the speed shown on screen — it is never saved, shared, or transmitted anywhere.

REQUIREMENTS
Requires precise (GPS) location permission and Google Play Services. Compatible with Android 11 and later. Android Auto support requires an Android Auto compatible car or head unit.

Tachimetro does one thing: show you how fast you're going, with nothing in the way.
