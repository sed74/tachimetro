What's new in version 1.1:

• Charging indicator: animated lightning icon next to the "Keep screen on" toggle, visible only while the phone is charging
• Trip distance: new field in the bottom-right corner, measured while the app is open and saved between sessions
• The "Reset" button now clears both max speed and distance
• No ads, no Internet connection
